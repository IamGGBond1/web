Strategy

We combine directional and relative-value ideas with a clear risk budget—size, liquidity, and drawdown tolerance are defined before expression.

  • Discretionary timing

    Entry, exit, and net exposure reflect the team’s read of regime, positioning, and asymmetry—not a single rigid model output.

  • Long-short structure

    The book is designed to isolate views from broad beta where appropriate, while remaining pragmatic on borrow, basis, and implementation.

  • Risk governance

    Limits, stress scenarios, and liquidity ladders are reviewed continuously; transparency for partners is non-negotiable.
