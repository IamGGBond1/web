并购与事件驱动 · 2026年4月10日 · 首篇

8个月20%-50%定期存款的机会

An 8-Month, 20%–50% “Certificate-of-Deposit-Like” Opportunity

2026 年 2 月 5 日，美团宣布收购叮咚买菜（DDL）的大陆业务，总对价约 9.97 亿美元。在美股上市的 DDL 全资控股大陆运营主体，公司主要资产也集中于大陆的叮咚买菜业务；美团此次仅收购大陆业务，不构成对上市公司的私有化，因而一般不涉及因本单交易而整体退市。

消息公布后，DDL 股价不涨反跌。市场普遍担心：创始人团队在交易落地后可能再度创业，上市公司能否足额、及时向股东分红或回购，存在不确定性。

2 月 10 日，DDL 公告称：收购完成后，拟将不低于公司当时现金余额的 90%用于股份回购与/或现金分红。

3 月 27 日，董事会通过议案：收购完成后，在扣除与交易相关的成本及应付费用后，将公司现金余额中不少于 90% 用于股份回购计划和/或向股东派发股息；具体执行由董事会依绝对酌情权在认为适当且符合公司最佳利益的前提下推进。

在上述前提下，若粗略按 9.97 亿美元对价的约 90% 对应约 9 亿美元的可分配规模，对照当前约 6 亿美元市值，则名义上存在约 50% 的上行空间；若再按约 20% 的股息相关税负/预扣做折价，约 7.2 亿美元，对应约 20% 的上行空间。

主要风险仍是收购未能完成，或推进过程中遭遇反垄断等监管因素而搁置。创始人已公开发布离职相关表态，且此类交易事前多与监管沟通，失败概率偏低。个人给出买入评级；该机会相似于在未来约 8 个月内、名义收益率约 20%–50% 的「类定存」式回报。

English

On February 5, 2026, Meituan announced the acquisition of Dingdong Maicai’s (DDL) mainland China business for a total consideration of approximately US$997 million. DDL, listed in the United States, wholly owns its mainland operating entity, and its principal assets are concentrated in the mainland Dingdong Maicai business. Because Meituan is acquiring only the mainland business, the transaction does not constitute a privatization of the listed company and, in general, does not imply a full delisting solely on account of this deal.

After the announcement, DDL’s share price fell rather than rose. The market broadly worried that, once the deal closes, founders might start new ventures, and that it was uncertain whether the listed company would return cash to shareholders through dividends or buybacks in full and on time.

On February 10, DDL stated that, after completion of the acquisition, it intends to use not less than 90% of the company’s then cash balance for share repurchases and/or cash dividends.

On March 27, the board approved a resolution that, after the acquisition closes and after deducting transaction-related costs and expenses, not less than 90% of the company’s cash balance will be used for a share repurchase program and/or cash dividends to shareholders; execution will be subject to the board’s sole discretion where it deems appropriate and in the best interests of the company.

Under these premises, if one roughly equates ~90% of the US$997 million price to about US$900 million of potentially distributable scale and compares that to a current market capitalization of about US$600 million, there is nominally on the order of ~50% upside. Applying a simplified ~20% haircut for dividend-related taxes or withholding (which varies materially by investor and holding structure) yields about US$720 million, or on the order of ~20% upside relative to the same market cap.

The principal risks remain deal failure or regulatory obstacles such as antitrust review during the process. The founders have made public departure-related statements, and transactions of this kind are often pre-communicated with regulators; the author therefore views failure as relatively unlikely. On a personal basis, the author assigns a buy rating and likens the setup to an approximately eight-month, nominal 20%–50% “CD-like” return profile—while noting that merger arbitrage is not a bank deposit and carries materially different legal, regulatory, and execution risks.
